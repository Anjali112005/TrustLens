# TrustLens 🔎

### AI-Powered Multimodal Trust Investigator

> **Don’t trust the name. Investigate the evidence.**

TrustLens is a phone-first, multimodal AI investigation platform designed to help students, job seekers, and professionals investigate internships, job offers, event invitations, companies, recruiters, websites, and payment requests.

Instead of checking only whether a company exists, TrustLens connects and cross-checks multiple pieces of evidence to determine whether a **specific opportunity or request is actually connected to the claimed company**.

---

## 🚨 Why TrustLens?

People regularly receive:

- Internship offers
- Job opportunities
- Recruitment messages
- Event invitations
- Registration links
- Payment requests
- QR codes
- Recruiter communications

These can appear legitimate because scammers may use real company names, logos, websites, fake recruiters, offer letters, and payment links.

A company can be legally registered while a particular internship, recruiter, or payment request associated with it may still be fraudulent.

TrustLens addresses this problem by bringing multiple verification steps into a single phone-first investigation workflow.

---

## 💡 Inspiration

TrustLens was inspired by a real-world internship scam where an individual paid money for an internship and only later discovered that the opportunity was fraudulent.

This highlighted a simple but important problem:

> **People don't just need to know whether a company exists. They need to know whether the specific opportunity in front of them can be trusted.**

TrustLens is designed around this idea.
